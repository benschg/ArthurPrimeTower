# Tenant profiles

Companion to [tenants.md](tenants.md). Researched 29 September 2026 from company websites, the Swiss commercial register (via Moneyhouse), Swiss Prime Site releases and Swiss press. "Not published" means it was searched for and not found. Logos and feature images for every entry are in `public/tenants/` (see `src/data/tenantAssets.ts`; regenerate with `node scripts/fetch-tenant-assets.mjs`).

Background: Swiss Prime Site's July 2010 pre-letting list (68 % let) already named Homburger, Citibank, Swiss & Global (later GAM) and Zürcher Kantonalbank. Deutsche Bank signed in December 2010. The 2011 opening press kit put the tenant mix at 44 % audit, consulting and law firms and 33 % financial services.

## Corrections to tenants.md

These findings contradict or sharpen the main list:

- **Nexxiot has left.** The register and website now give Nordstrasse 15, 8006 Zürich. An older ISO 27001 listing had Hardstrasse 201.
- **Trammo is gone.** Trammochem AG was deleted in 2013; its successor Trammo GmbH sits in Altendorf SZ. Only a lapsed LEI record still shows Hardstrasse 201.
- **Infosys tenancy is unconfirmed.** No release or register entry names a Prime Tower address; only the January 2026 move to The Circle is documented.
- **Equans is a service provider, not a tenant.** It runs reception and facility management; no branch is registered at Hardstrasse 201.
- **Migrolino is in the Cubus** (Hardstrasse 221), not the tower. Whether it replaced Coop Pronto at the same address is unconfirmed.
- **Floors now known:** Cognizant on 18 (since 2017), JLL expanded on 13 (2022), FlexOffice on 3 and 4 (since March 2023), Homburger reception on 31.
- **Zalando shrank.** Planned for 150+ staff, the Zurich hub had about 40 people in October 2024.
- **Universal Music** moved its registered address to the tower in autumn 2023.
- **Clouds** is on floor 35. No source confirms a separate conference floor on 34.
- **Rivington's** current website is rivington.ch ("RIVINGTON | Bar & Cafe").

## Prime Tower, office tenants

### Homburger AG
- Leading Swiss corporate law firm (M&A, capital markets, banking, litigation, tax). Founded 1957 by Prof. Eric Homburger, partner-owned, 41 partners and more than 160 lawyers.
- Anchor tenant, signed before completion. Eight floors with its own Gigon/Guyer fit-out; reception on 31.
- Dieter Gericke became Managing Partner in 2025. Several 2025 awards, including Legalcommunity CH Capital Markets Law Firm of the Year for the third year running.
- Web: https://www.homburger.ch · Sources: [SPS topping-out release 2010](https://sps.swiss/fileadmin/user_upload/redakteure/gruppe/pdf/pressemitteilungen/en/2010-07-07---Prime-Tower---topping-out-ceremony-for-Switzerlands-highe.pdf), [new managing partner](https://www.homburger.ch/en/news/dieter-gericke-new-managing-partner-of-homburger/)

### Deutsche Bank (Schweiz) AG and DWS CH AG
- Swiss arm of Deutsche Bank: wealth management, corporate and investment banking, plus DWS asset management. About 600 staff in Zurich and Geneva.
- Signed in December 2010 for more than 5,000 m², citing the space concept and LEED. Drees & Sommer fit-out targeting LEED Gold; moved in around December 2011. Later reduced from five floors to three.
- Clemens Kaiser became Chief Country Officer in March 2025 with a goal to double Swiss clients and revenue in five years.
- Web: https://country.db.com/switzerland · Sources: [Drees & Sommer fit-out](https://dreso.com/de/en/projects/details/tenant-fitout-prime-tower-deutsche-bank), [finews on new CEO](https://www.finews.ch/news/banken/66931-deutsche-bank-schweiz-new-ceo-clemens-kaiser-zurich-swiss-finance-place)

### Citibank (Switzerland) AG
- Citigroup's Swiss bank for corporate, institutional and private-bank clients. In Switzerland since 1963; about 400 staff in Switzerland and Monaco (2020).
- Pre-let tenant. In 2011 about 300 workplaces moved into four floors; refitted in 2014 (about 4,000 m²). Floor numbers not published.
- Country Officer Marni McManus since 2022.
- Web: https://www.citigroup.com/global/about-us/global-presence/switzerland · Sources: [Emch+Berger move](https://www.emchberger.ch/de/move-prime-tower?division=71), [finews](https://www.finews.ch/news/banken/47715-citi-ernennt-neue-schweiz-chefin)

### GAM Investments (GAM Holding AG)
- Independent asset manager, founded 1983, SIX-listed. Assets under management CHF 12.7 bn (June 2026), about 227 staff.
- Registered seat Hardstrasse 201. Predecessor Swiss & Global Asset Management was an opening tenant in 2011.
- Turnaround backed by Xavier Niel's NJJ; CEO Albert Saporta. FY2025 net loss CHF 74.2 m.
- Web: https://www.gam.com · Sources: [FY2025 results](https://www.boerse-online.de/dpa-afx/gnw-news-gam-gibt-ergebnisse-fuer-das-gesamtjahr-2025-bekannt-512559.html), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GAM_(company))

### Crypto Finance AG
- FINMA-licensed crypto broker, custodian and asset manager for institutions. Founded 2017 in Zug; majority-owned by Deutsche Börse since 2021; more than 100 staff.
- Headquarters on floor 24 since 2022. More than 90 % of staff work in Zurich.
- CEO Stijn Vander Straeten since November 2023.
- Web: https://www.crypto-finance.com · Sources: [about](https://www.crypto-finance.com/about-us/), [finews 2026](https://www.finews.ch/news/finanzplatz/73637-stijn-vander-straeten-cryptofinance-bitcoin-etp-micar-blackrock-verwahrung-kryptobranche-deutscheboerse-stablecoins-cbdc)

### Zalando Switzerland AG
- Zalando's Zurich tech hub for size-and-fit and recommendation systems, built on the ETH spin-off Fision acquired in 2020.
- Leased floor 23 and half of floor 7 (1,700 m²) in October 2021 for 150+ staff; moved in September 2022. Zalando's own blog cited about 40 employees in October 2024.
- Web: https://jobs.zalando.com/en/where-we-work/zurich · Sources: [Netzwoche 2021](https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2021-10-15/zalando-zieht-in-zuercher-prime-tower-ein), [primetower.ch interview](https://www.primetower.ch/en/2022/08/5-questions-for-dunja-maria-bischof-site-operations-lead-at-zalando), [Zalando blog](https://jobs.zalando.com/en/blog/inside-zurich-tech-hub)

### HDI Global SE, Zurich branch
- Swiss branch of the Talanx group's industrial insurer: property, liability, marine and specialty lines. About 94 staff in Switzerland and Liechtenstein.
- Branch headquarters on floor 21. Move-in year not published.
- CEO Marc Luginbühl since 2020. Best Workplaces Switzerland 2024, rank 7.
- Web: https://www.hdi.global/ch/de/ · Sources: [Great Place to Work](https://greatplacetowork.ch/en/employer/hdi-global-se-zurich-branch-switzerland/), [Intelligent Insurer](https://www.intelligentinsurer.com/news/hdi-global-hires-zurich-exec-as-new-ceo-of-switzerland-22588)

### Oracle Software (Schweiz) GmbH
- Oracle's Swiss subsidiary, legal seat in Baden-Dättwil. The Zurich site hosts Oracle Labs (GraalVM) and a customer innovation space.
- The SMART Innovation Center opened on the whole of floor 17 on 17 April 2018, with about 50 researchers. Whether it is still there is not published.
- Web: https://www.oracle.com/ch-de/ · Sources: [Netzwoche 2018](https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2018-04-18/oracle-mietet-sich-im-prime-tower-ein)

### Roland Berger AG
- Swiss office of the Munich strategy consultancy (about 3,500 staff worldwide). Zurich is its only Swiss office.
- Moved from Seefeld in January 2021, going from five floors to one; about 70 staff. Partners cite transport links, a single floor and the mountain view.
- Web: https://www.rolandberger.com · Sources: [primetower.ch](https://www.primetower.ch/en/2021/03/roland-berger-ag-our-new-arrival-at-the-prime-tower/)

### Repower AG, Zurich office
- Graubünden energy group (hydro, wind, solar, grids, trading), headquartered in Poschiavo. 747 staff; 2025 group profit CHF 101 m. Main shareholders EKZ and the canton of Graubünden.
- Zurich reception on floor 6. Functions and headcount not published.
- Web: https://www.repower.com · Sources: [Zurich directions sheet](https://repower.com/new-media/l25nsi1o/zuerich_2023_en.pdf)

### Korn Ferry
- US executive-search and organisational consulting firm. Swiss office opened 1987.
- Moved in on 2 April 2012 after doubling to about 50 staff; Zurich was to become its European headquarters.
- Web: https://www.kornferry.com · Sources: [moneycab 2012](https://www.moneycab.com/?p=1116197)

### JLL (Jones Lang LaSalle) AG
- Swiss arm of the real-estate services firm, founded in 2011 by Jan Eckert with 11 staff. Also the tower's letting agent.
- Moved in November 2011; expanded on floor 13 in 2022. About 40 of its 60 Swiss staff work in the tower.
- Web: https://www.jll.ch · Sources: [primetower.ch](https://www.primetower.ch/?p=1050), [immobilienbusiness](https://www.immobilienbusiness.ch/en/?p=54206)

### Cognizant Technology Solutions AG
- Swiss unit of the US IT-services group (USD 21.1 bn revenue 2025). Legal seat Baar; Zurich branch since 2003.
- Zurich office moved to floor 18 in 2017. Cognizant Netcentric also lists Prime Tower as its Zurich hub.
- Web: https://www.cognizant.com/ch/ · Sources: [20 years in Switzerland](https://www.cognizant.com/ch/en/about-cognizant/20-years-cognizant-switzerland), [Moneyhouse](https://www.moneyhouse.ch/de/company/cognizant-technology-solutions-ag-zweignieder-20466565121)

### Nexxiot AG (moved out)
- Zurich IoT firm tracking rail wagons and containers; about 120 staff. Knorr-Bremse is the largest shareholder.
- Formerly at Hardstrasse 201; now Nordstrasse 15, 8006 Zürich. CEO Maximilian Eichhorn since June 2024.

### Swiss Prime Site Immobilien AG and Swiss Prime Site Solutions AG

- Web: https://sps.swiss · Sources: [Moneyhouse](https://www.moneyhouse.ch/de/company/swiss-prime-site-immobilien-ag-2865116581), [H1 2026 results](https://live.deutsche-boerse.com/nachrichten/1H26-Swiss-Prime-Site-mit-starkem-operativem-Ergebnis-und-nachhaltigem-Wachstum-FFO-I-von-CHF-215-pro-Aktie-24-bfab409b-06c5-4055-a924-f8170d8776af)

### FlexOffice (Schweiz) AG

## Prime Tower, ground floor, top floor and services

### Clouds
- Restaurant, bistro, bar and event room on floor 35, about 120 m up. Kitchen 100 seats, bistro 60, bar 70 standing, Privé 40.
- Opened 12 December 2011. Candrian Catering has run it since 1 July 2015. Falstaff named the bar among Switzerland's best in 2026.
- Web: https://www.clouds.ch · Sources: [zuerich.com](https://www.zuerich.com/de/besuchen/bars-lounges/clouds-bar-bistro), [event venue data](https://www.eventlokale.ch/site/_eventlokale/1/Schweiz/43378/clouds_ihr_eventlokal_im_prime_tower.html)

### Zürcher Kantonalbank
- Moved in June 2011 with a branch, private banking and about 100 asset-management staff on floors 3 and 4 (later about 250).
- Announced in 2017 that it would give up its 3,800 m² of offices by 2020. Only the branch remains.
- Web: https://www.zkb.ch/de/standorte/zuerich-primetower.html · Sources: [Inside Paradeplatz 2017](https://insideparadeplatz.ch/2017/06/30/zkb-verlaesst-prime-tower-cs-geht-raus-aus-leu-haus/)

### Hotel Rivington & Sons
- Two-level cocktail bar and café in the tower lobby styled as a 1930s New York speakeasy, with a 1930s bar counter and six tonnes of subway tiles shipped from New York.
- Run by Raumzuerich GmbH, the team behind La Stanza. The NZZ profiled it in 2024.
- Web: https://rivington.ch · Sources: [zuerich.com](https://www.zuerich.com/de/besuchen/bars-lounges/hotel-rivington-sons), [NZZ](https://www.nzz.ch/gesellschaft/ein-hotel-ohne-bett-muss-keine-mogelpackung-sein-ld.1821598)

### Migrolino
- Migros convenience store "migrolino Zürich Prime Tower", actually at Hardstrasse 221 in the Cubus. Open Monday to Saturday from 06:00.
- Web: https://www.migrolino.ch/de/standorte/migrolino-zurich-primetower

### Wincasa AG
- Real-estate service provider with about 1,000 staff. Bought by Swiss Prime Site in 2012 and sold to Implenia in 2023.
- Manages the site: operating costs, service charges and steering the facility-management provider.
- Web: https://www.wincasa.ch · Sources: [Wincasa Prime Tower page](https://www.wincasa.ch/en-ch/prime-tower), [SPS sale release](https://sps.swiss/en/group/media/media-releases/overview/media-release-detail/swiss-prime-site-to-sell-wincasa-to-implenia)
